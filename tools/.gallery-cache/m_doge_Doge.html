Enemy[]

Doge is the first enemy, the main and only opponent faced in Korea/Nagasaki. In almost all stages he appears in, he is used as a meatshield for stronger enemies of all sorts. Do not fear this enemy because he can be defeated with anything you use for more significant enemies.

Strategy[]

With his low stats, Doge can easily be killed by almost anything, even by low-level Normal Cats. Although he can be ignored most of the time, be aware of his buffs in Way of the Ninja and The Noble Tribe. In later encounters, the use of Wave Attack Cats like Crazed Gross Cat and Slime Cat is recommended to clear waves of Doges, in lieu of Single Target Cats or others who may miss because of a Doge getting in the way, although this is far from necessary. Doge often serves to take hits meant for stronger enemies, like backliners such as Dark Emperor Nyandam, or pushers like R.Ost.

Trivia[]

  • His English name references the eponymous Shiba Inu that became a meme on the internet.
  • His Japanese name, Wan-ko (Doggy/Puppy), is the dog equivalent to Nyan-ko (Kitty) in that the onomatopoeia for a dog's bark is 'wan' and a cat's meow is 'nyan'. The 'ko' is just a suffix meaning child.
  • Nyanko New Japan and Battle Cats Quest are the only Battle Cats games not to include any variation of Doge.
  • The Birthday Cake enemy may be a reference to how Doge sends gifts to Cat annually.
  • According to several descriptions, Doge and his variants are part of a larger Doge clan.
    • Gabriel is the great-grandfather of the Doge clan, Zoge is Doge's late father, Aku Doge and Wild Doge are Doge's brothers, Li'l Doge is Doge's time-traveling grandchild from the future, an unnamed aunt is mentioned in Miss Haka's Japanese description, and King Wahwah is described as "Head of the Doge Dynasty" in the event poster for King Wahwah's Revenge.
  • In BCEN, his gender is confirmed in Li'l Doge's description, where it's mentioned that Doge is Li'l Doge's grandfather in the future.
    • In BCJP, Doge was previously established as male in Aku Doge's description, which refers to Doge as his younger brother.
  • He is the enemy with the most variants.
  • With the addition of Gigahaniwan in Version 13.3, Doge became the first and only enemy to have a variant for every non-collab enemy type.
  • He is one of the three enemies to have both unstarred and Starred Alien variants, the others being Gory and the Cyclones.
