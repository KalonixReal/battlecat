Gross Cat (Normal Cat)

78
 Sign In to Save Save View source
  • History
  • Purge
  • Talk (0)

Gross Cat is one of the Normal Cats, and the first ranged attacker the player gets. He is unlocked after clearing Philippines in Empire of Cats Chapter 1 and costs 0 XP or a Normal Cat Capsule to unlock. True Form gains increased heaIth, attack power and strong against Aliens. In The Battle Cats Unite!, it is unlocked from clearing Philippines in the Empire of Cats Chapter and costs 3000 XP or rolling the Normal Cat Capsule.

Strategy/Usage[]

Gross Cat helps out in a big way against enemies early on with high health and attack power, as he can outrange them. For example, enemies like Hippoe and Gory have almost no defense against a well-protected army of Gross Cats. Gross Cat decreases in use through the Stories of Legend stages with the increased amounts of very long-ranged enemies, especially if the player already has strong Area Attack units like Paris Cat or Drama Cats.

Gross Cat can be extremely useful at holding his position when stacked and behind an array of Tank Cats, due to the Gross Cat's decent attack power and the Tank Cats' extreme health. While doing this, you can level up your Worker Cat and build up money for a big push.

True Form[]

Macho Leg Cat changes his focus to being an all-rounder unit. His health, attack, range, cooldown, attack speed, movement speed and cost are quite good; he has no real weak point. Macho Leg Cat also has notably higher health than many ranged Cats, even more than Eraser Cat when hypermaxed, all while staying at the relatively cheap cost of 600¢, making Macho a good unit for money-intensive stages early on.

Later into the game, Macho can be used as a cheaper replacement for Lizard Cat due to his shorter cooldown time, in trade-off for less damage, range and health, which is an ideal trade-off in some stages. Furthermore, Macho Leg Cat can be very useful in stages with Alien enemies, because of his high health and increased effectiveness against them.

Unfortunately, Macho begins to lose his relevance later on. Alternatives like Cameraman Cat, Paris Cat, Pizza Cat, Can Can Cat can offer better performance and have their own advantages that each can outclass it, most prominently in Uncanny Legends, Slapstick Cats are more viable in most stages. Even worse for Macho, most mid-to-late-Stories of Legend and Uncanny Legends enemies often deal extreme damage, further reducing his utility as he would then be a waste of cash.

Appearance[]

  • Normal Form: Looks like a Cat with very, very long legs, making him over twice as tall as a basic Cat.
  • Evolved Form: Now has two breathtakingly beautiful and slender human legs. Attacks by kicking and showing off his shapely legs. Cool fit for Actress Cat.
  • True Form: Sexy Legs Cat went to the gym! His legs become incredibly muscular and buff, now with toes. A small vein can be seen just above Macho Leg Cat's face. Aesthetic fit for Beefcake Cat.

Trivia[]

  • Version 3.0 of The Battle Cats gave his True Form an anti-Alien ability, specifically Strong Against.
  • Prior to Version 4.7, Sexy Legs Cat was a part of three other Cat Combos: Cat Army MkII, Maxed Out and Cover Girl.
  • Unlike all other variants of this Cat, Macho Leg Cat doesn't use plural for "Leg" in its name.
  • There is a typo in the Normal Form description saying "Able to do very a long range attack" when it should be "Able to do a very long range attack".
  • Gross Cat is one of the two Normal Cats that have a faster attack animation and speed in their Evolved and True Forms than in their Normal Forms, the other being Lizard Cat.
  • He and Bird Cat have different animations in forms.
  • Variants of Salon Cat usually have descriptions referencing Sexy Legs/Macho Leg Cat, as when combined, they appear humanoid.
  • Sexy Legs Cat has the 2nd most variants in the game, behind the titular Cat.
